
when distributed under the public AGPL licensing path.

Commercially licensed copies are governed by the applicable commercial
agreement rather than by the SPDX notice alone.

## Important contributor note

Dual licensing works cleanly only if the project has the legal right to offer
the relevant code under both licensing paths. Before accepting outside
contributions, establish contribution terms (for example, a CLA or copyright
assignment) that preserve the project's ability to issue commercial licenses.
